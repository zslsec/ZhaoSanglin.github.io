**Abstract**
This paper investigates whether local governments walk the talk by examining the relationship between environmental policy expression, its semantic structure, and green productivity transformation. Using text analysis of Chinese local government policy documents combined with regional green productivity measures, the study reveals gaps between policy rhetoric and actual environmental outcomes, offering implications for policy design and governance transparency.
